The Platform

Introducing AIIS

AI Integrity Software — one unified platform that connects every aspect of your asset integrity program.

AIIS is AI Integrity's proprietary software platform, purpose-built for the oil & gas, petrochemical, and heavy industrial sectors. It brings together six deeply integrated modules under a single web-based environment — eliminating data silos and giving every stakeholder a real-time, unified view of asset health.

Whether you're an integrity engineer reviewing inspection history, a corrosion specialist tracking anomalies, or a plant manager monitoring operational KPIs — AIIS puts the right information in the right hands, at the right time.

Web-Based & Accessible Anywhere No local installation required. Access AIIS from desktop, tablet, or field device through any modern browser.
Role-Based Access Control Granular permissions for engineers, inspectors, managers, and auditors — each seeing exactly what they need.
Multi-Site & Multi-Asset Support Manage hundreds of assets across multiple facilities from a single platform instance.
Live Dashboards & Automated Reports Real-time KPI dashboards and scheduled report generation keep all stakeholders informed automatically.
Full Audit Trail & Compliance Every action is logged with timestamps and user attribution — supporting API, ASME, and regulatory audits.
AI-Powered Chat Assistant Ask questions in plain language — query asset status, risk levels, anomalies, and KPIs instantly without navigating menus.
Try the AI Assistant Ask "Which assets are high risk?" or "Show overdue inspections" — get instant answers from your integrity data.

Our Platform Modules

Six Integrated Modules,
One Unified Platform

Every module is purpose-built for asset integrity — connected, intelligent, and actionable.

01

Asset Datasheet

A centralised digital repository of all asset-specific information — from equipment specifications and technical datasheets to integrity parameters and supporting reference documents.

Contents
  • Asset Register & Tagging
  • Equipment Technical Datasheet
  • Integrity Parameters
  • Document Library
Learn more →
02

Risk Based Inspection (RBI)

Structured risk ranking of all equipment using qualitative, semi-quantitative, and quantitative methodologies — aligned with API 580/581 and industry best practice.

Assessment Methods
  • Qualitative RBI
  • Semi-Quantitative RBI
  • Quantitative RBI
Learn more →
03

Inspection Management

End-to-end management of your entire inspection lifecycle — from scheduling through execution to centralized record-keeping of all footage and reports.

Includes
  • Inspection Schedule
  • Inspection History
  • Inspection Footage Database
  • Inspection Report Database
Learn more →
04

Anomaly Management

Capture, track, and resolve every detected anomaly in a structured workflow — ensuring no defect is left unaddressed and every resolution is fully documented.

Workflow Stages
  • Anomaly Record & Classification
  • Anomaly Status Tracking
  • Anomaly Resolution & Close-out
Learn more →
05

Engineering Assessment

Rigorous fitness-for-service and remaining-life calculations performed by qualified engineers using AI-augmented models, corrosion analysis, and material evaluation.

Capabilities
  • Fitness-for-Service (FFS)
  • Remaining Life Calculation
  • Corrosion & Degradation Analysis
  • Material & Weld Assessment
Learn more →
06

Operational KPI

Live operational dashboards connecting production performance with asset integrity data — giving engineers a complete picture of how operations impact asset health.

KPI Metrics
  • Production Rate Trend
  • Temperature History
  • Pressure History
  • Corrosion Inhibitor Performance
